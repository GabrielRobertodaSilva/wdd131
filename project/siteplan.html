Jiu Jitsu Club - Site Plan

Gabriel Roberto da Silva

WDD131 - Web Frontend Development I

1. Site Name

Name: Jiu Jitsu Club

Reasoning: The name is direct, inclusive, and easy to remember. It establishes a sense of community for practitioners and enthusiasts looking for a reliable hub dedicated to the history, structure, and current events of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

Optional Domain Availability: jiujitsuclub.org

2. Site Purpose

Purpose Statement: Jiu Jitsu Club is an educational web platform designed to explore the origins and rich history of Jiu-Jitsu. The site features dedicated sections for the belt ranking system, a directory of academies, and an updated calendar of upcoming competitions.

Personal Motivation: As a Jiu-Jitsu practitioner, I truly love this sport. I created this project because I believe practitioners and enthusiasts are always seeking a deeper connection, accurate knowledge, and a reliable place to explore the history, belt progression, and local Jiu-Jitsu events.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "How did Jiu-Jitsu originate, and what are the rules and time requirements to progress through the belt ranking system?"
  • Scenario 2: "Where can I find registered Jiu-Jitsu academies nearby and check the calendar for upcoming regional competitions?"

4. Color Palette

The color scheme uses deep navy for tradition and discipline, vibrant gold for achievements/competitions, and clean neutral tones for readable educational content.

Primary Color #1a2a3a (Header, Navigation, & Major Headings)
Secondary Color #c99700 (Buttons, Highlights, & Accents)
Background Color #f4f6f8 (Main Page Background)
Text Color #212529 (Body Text)

5. Typography

Heading Font: 'Montserrat', sans-serif (Bold and modern aesthetic suitable for sports and martial arts titles).

Body Font: 'Open Sans', sans-serif (Highly readable font for long articles on history and rules).

Heading Example: Montserrat Bold

Body Text Example: Open Sans Regular. Jiu-Jitsu is a martial art based on leverage, technique, and continuous learning both on and off the mat.
